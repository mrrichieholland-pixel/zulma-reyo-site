INNER ALCHEMY

This is a place for those who wish to deepen their knowledge of Inner Alchemy, and a space in which to explore questions arising from your experience with its main tool, the Alchemical Alignment. It is meant to inspire you to be your own teacher, evoking transmutation and perception, interconnecting multidimensional realities, and aiming towards a deeper integration of personality and soul — a process that has affinities with what Jung called individuation.

HELPING TO PRESERVE THE INNER ALCHEMY LEGACY

The Inner Alchemy Archive is being created from more than fifty years of written, audio and visual material. This is a substantial undertaking, and I welcome the help of those who feel genuinely connected to the work and would like to contribute to preserving it for the future.

There may be opportunities to assist with organising, identifying, transcribing, cataloguing and preparing material for the Archive. If you have relevant skills, knowledge of Inner Alchemy, or simply feel called to help with this work, you are invited to contact us.

The intention is to make the principles, practices and history of Inner Alchemy increasingly available to those who wish to explore them, now and in the years to come.
