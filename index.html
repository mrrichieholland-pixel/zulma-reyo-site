ZULMA REYO

Welcome to my personal website.

Now that I have officially retired and am no longer teaching, I have decided to offer a space filled with Inner Alchemy material that was formerly available exclusively to students, for those who wish either to continue or to begin learning on their own.

It is important to me that this legacy be carried into the future and applied individually to all facets of life. With this in mind, the selection of texts is large and varied. I am sure you will find your niche in one or other of the articles, manuals, digital books or downloadable materials that I provide here, with more to be added gradually.

If you have a special interest or a subject you would like to know more about, please submit your request through the form here. Please ensure that all questions relate to the practice of Inner Alchemy.

As promised so many times, I am here and will continue to be here for you, in person and through the legacy I leave for the future.

Fondly,

ZULMA

A SOUL'S JOURNEY

In A Soul’s Journey, Zulma Reyo shares the story of a life lived between worlds: the outer life of the woman, teacher and seeker, and the inner life of memory, revelation and spiritual recognition.

Through the voices of Zulma, Ameliah, and Sa’arah, the book moves beyond personal testament into a profound exploration of personality, soul, and Spirit.

Winner of the Mind, Body, Spirit category at the Independent Press Awards 2022 and winner of Gold in the Body, Mind & Spirit Practices category at the Nautilus Book Awards 2022.
