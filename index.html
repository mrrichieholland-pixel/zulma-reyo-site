ZULMA REYO

Welcome to my personal website.

For more than fifty years, Inner Alchemy has been at the centre of my life and work. During that time I have explored, practised and shared its principles with people who have come from many different places, each bringing their own questions, experiences and search for greater understanding.

Today, at 83, I find myself looking back over that long journey and, at the same time, looking forward. I am gathering what has been learned, rediscovering material from the past, completing new books and making more of the Inner Alchemy work available to those who wish to explore it for themselves.

This website is part of that process.

Here you will find my books and writings, meditations, videos and other material from the Inner Alchemy Archive as it develops. Above all, I hope it will encourage you to deepen your own experience of the Alchemical Alignment and to discover what Inner Alchemy can reveal to you.

After all these years, I have come to understand something very clearly: Inner Alchemy cannot truly be taught. Its principles and practices can be shared, but the real teaching happens within you.
My task is to point towards the Open Door.
It is for you to enter.
