ABOUT THE BOOK

The book is divided into several parts: first, it explores the basic human energetic anatomy and the subtle powers that we possess, so that we may understand how to manifest and effect change. Second, it explores the energetic anatomy of the world around us, that is, the different dimensions that make up our reality and how we might access them. The final part of the book induces us to reflect on and use this knowledge for real action in the world. This is a book of experiential, not conceptual, spirituality, and therefore exercises, visualisations, and meditations are provided throughout the text.
